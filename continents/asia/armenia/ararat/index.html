Ararat's Hidden Gems

HISTORY

Ararat, a biblical and historic mountain, is traditionally identified as the resting place of Noah's Ark. Located in modern-day Turkey, near the borders of Armenia and Iran, it holds cultural and religious significance, particularly in Armenian mythology and Christianity. The mountain symbolizes Armenian identity and resilience.























CULTURE

Culture encompasses the shared beliefs, values, customs, traditions, language, art, and practices of a group or society. It shapes identities, influences behavior, and is transmitted across generations. Culture evolves over time, adapting to changes while preserving core elements that define a community’s way of life and worldview.
