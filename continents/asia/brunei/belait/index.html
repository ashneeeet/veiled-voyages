Belait's Hidden Gems

HISTORY

Belait’s history was transformed by the 1929 discovery of oil in Seria, evolving from a quiet fishing region into Brunei’s industrial backbone. Originally a small settlement at the river's mouth, it became a strategic administrative hub under British influence, later witnessing significant growth through post-war reconstruction and offshore gas development.























CULTURE

The culture of Belait is a rich tapestry of indigenous heritage and modern industrial influence. It is home to the Belait, Iban, and Dusun peoples, whose traditional longhouse lifestyles and crafts coexist with a diverse expatriate community. Islamic traditions, vibrant night markets, and unique local dialects define its social soul.
