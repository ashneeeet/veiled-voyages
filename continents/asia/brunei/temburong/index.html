Temburong's Hidden Gems

HISTORY

Once part of the mighty Bruneian Empire, Temburong was geographically severed in 1890 when Charles Brooke annexed neighboring Limbang into Sarawak. Remaining a secluded, rainforest-covered Bruneian exclave accessible only by boat or foreign transit, it finally reconnected directly with mainland Brunei in 2020 through the landmark Temburong Bridge.























CULTURE

Temburong’s culture is a peaceful blend of traditional Bruneian Malay customs and indigenous Iban and Murut heritage. Life revolves around Islamic values, close-knit community gatherings, and deep respect for nature. Locals preserve traditional longhouse living, intricate handicrafts, and river-based lifestyles, celebrating vibrant festivals like Hari Raya and Gawai Dayak.
