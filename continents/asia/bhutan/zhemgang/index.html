Zhemgang's Hidden Gems

HISTORY

Zhemgang, a district in central Bhutan, is rich in cultural and natural heritage. Historically, it was part of the Kheng region, known for its distinct Khengpa culture. The area has deep ties to Buddhism, with ancient monasteries and sacred sites. Its dense forests and wildlife make it ecologically significant.























CULTURE

Zhemgang's culture is rooted in Khengpa traditions, featuring colorful Tshechus with sacred mask dances. Locals preserve heritage through folk songs, stories, and rituals. Handicrafts like bamboo weaving and textiles thrive. Buddhism influences spiritual practices, with ancient monasteries and festivals fostering unity, faith, and cultural identity in the region.
