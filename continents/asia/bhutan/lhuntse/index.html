Lhuntse's Hidden Gems

HISTORY

Lhuntse, a remote district in Bhutan, is known for its spiritual and cultural significance. It is the ancestral home of Bhutan's royal family and renowned for its intricate textiles. Historically isolated, the district's spiritual legacy is rooted in sacred sites like Lhuntse Dzong, built in 1654 as a fortress and monastery.























CULTURE

Lhuntse, Bhutan, boasts a rich culture deeply rooted in spirituality, art, and tradition. Its renowned textiles, particularly Kushuthara (intricate handwoven patterns), highlight its artistic heritage. The district celebrates traditional festivals like Lhuntse Tshechu, featuring masked dances and rituals. Sacred monasteries and strong Buddhist traditions shape its serene and vibrant cultural identity.
